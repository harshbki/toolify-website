Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

Welcome to Toolify. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data within our mobile application available on the Indus Appstore.

Data Safety Promise: Toolify is built on a "Privacy First" architecture. We do not collect, store, or share your personal files, notes, or images on any external servers.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Toolify processes all user data locally on the device. When you use the PDF tools, Image Compressor, or Notes, the data stays on your phone. We do not have access to your private documents.

2. App Permissions

  • Camera: Used strictly for scanning QR codes and capturing photos for image-to-PDF conversion.
  • Storage (Files and Media): Required to save your generated PDFs and access images you wish to edit/compress.
  • Internet: Required for AI features and displaying advertisements.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob for monetization. AdMob may collect device identifiers and approximate location to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

4. Third-Party AI Services

AI features (like Text Generation and Grammar Check) use secure APIs. Any data sent to these services is processed ephemerally and is not stored by Toolify.
